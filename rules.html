School Rules

Discipline • Responsibility • Excellence

General Conduct

  • Students must respect teachers, staff, and fellow learners.
  • Punctuality is required for all classes, assemblies, and activities.
  • Bullying, fighting, or use of abusive language is strictly prohibited.
  • Respect for school property and environment is mandatory.
  • Mobile phones and unauthorized electronic devices are not allowed.

Academic Rules

  • Students must attend all lessons and complete assignments on time.
  • Cheating in examinations or tests will lead to disciplinary action.
  • Library books must be returned on time and handled with care.
  • Classroom discipline must be maintained at all times.

Dress Code

  • Full school uniform must be worn during school hours.
  • Games kit is compulsory during sports activities.
  • Students must keep uniforms neat, clean, and properly branded.
  • Jewelry, makeup, or unauthorized clothing is not permitted.

Boarding Rules

  • Lights out at 10:00 PM; silence must be observed thereafter.
  • Students must maintain cleanliness in dormitories and bathrooms.
  • Cooking in dormitories is strictly prohibited.
  • Visitors are only allowed with permission from the administration.
  • Students must respect prefects and dorm supervisors.
