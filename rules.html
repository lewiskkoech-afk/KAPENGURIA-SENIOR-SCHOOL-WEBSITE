School Rules

Discipline • Excellence • Success

General Conduct

  • Students must uphold discipline, respect, and integrity at all times.
  • Punctuality is mandatory for all lessons, assemblies, and school events.
  • Respect for teachers, staff, and fellow students is expected.
  • Bullying, fighting, or harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited.

Academic Rules

  • Students must attend all classes and complete assignments on time.
  • Cheating in examinations or tests will result in disciplinary action.
  • Library books must be returned on time and handled with care.

Dress Code

  • Students must wear the prescribed school uniform at all times.
  • Games kits and tracksuits are compulsory during sports activities.
  • Neat grooming and personal hygiene are expected daily.

Boarding Rules

  • Silence must be observed in dormitories after lights out.
  • Students must keep their dormitories clean and orderly.
  • Unauthorized items such as radios, phones, or sharp objects are not allowed.

Disciplinary Measures

Any violation of school rules will attract disciplinary action, which may include warnings, detention, suspension, or expulsion depending on the severity of the offense.
