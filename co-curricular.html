Co-Curricular Activities

Developing talents • Building teamwork • Inspiring leadership

Sports

Kapenguria School has a proud tradition of sporting excellence. Students participate in:

  • Football – County Champions 2026
  • Athletics – Gold medalists in regional competitions
  • Basketball – National playoff qualifiers
  • Volleyball – Strong contenders in inter-school tournaments

Clubs & Societies

Our clubs and societies nurture talents and leadership skills:

  • Science & Innovation Club – National Science Congress finalists
  • Debate & Drama Club – County Debate Champions
  • Environmental Conservation Club – Tree planting and clean-up drives
  • Christian Union & Young Catholic Students – Spiritual growth and fellowship

Arts & Culture

Students showcase creativity and cultural heritage through:

  • Drama festivals – Award-winning performances
  • Music – Choir and band presentations at county level
  • Traditional dance – Preserving local culture and identity

Leadership Activities

Leadership is cultivated through active participation in:

  • Student Council – Representing learners’ voices
  • Prefect System – Discipline and responsibility
  • Peer Mentorship – Guiding fellow students academically and socially

Achievements

Our co-curricular programs have produced outstanding results:

  • Winners of County Drama Festival 2025
  • Regional Athletics Champions 2026
  • National Debate finalists 2025
  • Environmental Club recognized for tree planting initiatives
