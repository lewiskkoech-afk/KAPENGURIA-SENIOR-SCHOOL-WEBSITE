Academics

Excellence in Learning • Discipline in Study • Success in Results

Academic Departments

Kapenguria School offers a wide range of subjects across key departments:

  • Sciences – Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • Mathematics – Pure and Applied
  • Languages – English, Kiswahili, French
  • Humanities – History, Geography, CRE
  • Technical Subjects – Business Studies, Agriculture, Computer Studies

Curriculum

The school follows the Kenyan 8-4-4 curriculum, preparing students for KCSE examinations. Emphasis is placed on both academic excellence and holistic development, ensuring learners are well-rounded and ready for future challenges.

Academic Performance

Kapenguria School has consistently ranked among the top schools in West Pokot County. Recent highlights include:

  • KCSE 2025 mean score of 9.2 (B+).
  • Over 80% of candidates qualifying for university admission.
  • Top performers in Mathematics and Sciences at county level.

Support Programs

To enhance academic success, the school provides:

  • Evening and weekend remedial classes.
  • Mentorship programs led by alumni and teachers.
  • Access to a modern library and ICT lab.
  • Guidance and counseling services for students.
