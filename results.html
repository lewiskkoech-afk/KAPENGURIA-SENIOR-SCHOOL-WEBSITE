KCSE Results

Celebrating Academic Excellence

Overall Performance

Kapenguria School has consistently excelled in KCSE examinations. In 2025, the school achieved a mean score of 9.2 (B+), ranking among the top schools in West Pokot County.

  • Over 80% of candidates qualified for university admission.
  • Top student scored an A plain with 84 points.
  • 10 students scored A-, 25 scored B+, and majority attained strong passes.

Subject Analysis

Performance by subject in KCSE 2025:

  • Mathematics – Mean grade A-
  • English – Mean grade B+
  • Kiswahili – Mean grade B
  • Biology – Mean grade B+
  • Chemistry – Mean grade B+
  • Physics – Mean grade A-
  • History – Mean grade B+
  • Geography – Mean grade B+

University Placement

Our graduates have secured placements in top universities across Kenya and abroad:

  • University of Nairobi – Medicine, Engineering, Law
  • Kenyatta University – Education, Business Studies
  • Moi University – ICT, Agriculture, Nursing
  • International scholarships – 5 students admitted to universities in the USA and UK

Recent Achievements

Kapenguria School continues to shine in academics:

  • National Science Congress finalists in 2025.
  • County Debate Champions 2025.
  • Regional Mathematics Contest winners.
