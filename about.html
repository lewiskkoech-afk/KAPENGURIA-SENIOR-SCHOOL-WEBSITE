About Us

Shaping Future Leaders • Excellence • Discipline • Success

Our History

Kapenguria School, formerly Kapenguria Boys High School, was established to provide quality education to young men in West Pokot County. Over the years, it has grown into a center of academic excellence and holistic development, producing leaders who serve both locally and nationally.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a leading institution that nurtures disciplined, innovative, and responsible citizens.

Mission: To provide quality education and holistic training that empowers learners to excel academically, socially, and morally.

Core Values

  • Discipline – the foundation of success.
  • Excellence – striving for the highest standards.
  • Integrity – honesty and accountability in all actions.
  • Teamwork – working together for collective achievement.
  • Service – giving back to the community.

Achievements

Kapenguria School has consistently ranked among the top schools in West Pokot County. Our students have excelled in:

  • KCSE examinations with outstanding mean scores.
  • National Science Congress competitions.
  • County and regional sports championships.
  • Debate and drama festivals at national level.
  • Community service and outreach programs.

Principal’s Message

Welcome to Kapenguria School. Our institution is built on discipline, excellence, and service. We believe in nurturing students to become responsible citizens who will positively impact society. Together, we strive for success in academics, co-curricular activities, and personal growth.
