Students’ Arena

Empowering learners • Building leaders • Inspiring excellence

Student Welfare

Kapenguria School prioritizes the well-being of its students by providing:

  • Modern dormitories with adequate facilities.
  • Nutritious meals served daily in the dining hall.
  • Access to healthcare services within the school compound.
  • Guidance and counseling programs for emotional support.

Student Council

The Student Council represents the voice of learners and promotes leadership skills. Members are elected annually and work closely with administration to improve student life.

  • President – Oversees council activities.
  • Secretary – Handles communication and records.
  • Treasurer – Manages student projects and funds.
  • Class Representatives – Bridge between students and teachers.
