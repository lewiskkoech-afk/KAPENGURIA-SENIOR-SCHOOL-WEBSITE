Students’ Arena

Empowering learners with discipline, excellence, and success

Students' Welfare

At Kapenguria School, student welfare is at the heart of our mission. We foster discipline, teamwork, and leadership through active participation in school life and the student council.

The Student Council

The Student Council represents the voice of learners, ensuring that every student has a platform to contribute to the growth and success of the school. Guided by the Dean of Students, the council works tirelessly to uphold discipline, excellence, and unity.

Students' Council Chairman's Desk

A man once stood. A man with a dream. A man with vision. A man with a reason to live. Martin Luther King foresaw a greater future for the United States. I stand with a belief. I stand with hope. I stand awaiting greatness for our giant school.

For a long time we have said “Shine your light” but actually lived in darkness. I see the light, we see the light, and definitely we are at the end of the tunnel. No journey starts with a leap; not even the leap of faith as it must be preceded by a step. The common analogy: a journey of 1000 miles begins with a single step.

With a strong administration guiding us, superb teachers dedicating their time, and staff who look after us tirelessly, we are going to make it. We are still at the horizon, at sunrise as a school, but don't count us out yet. Let's meet at noon.

I am proud of being part of a team spearheading recovery, bringing back the light. I head a council of 52 councillors, patroned by our able Dean of Students Mr. Noah Tenal. It feels great to be a leader in a leading school. All hail the men in green, all hail.

Student Achievements

Kapenguria School students excel in academics, sports, and leadership. Recent highlights include:

  • Top KCSE performance in West Pokot County (2025).
  • Winners of the County Debate Championship.
  • Regional football champions – “Men in Green” team.
  • National Science Congress finalists in Physics and Biology.
  • Active participation in community service and outreach programs.
