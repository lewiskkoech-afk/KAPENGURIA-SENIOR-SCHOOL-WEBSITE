Admissions

Kapenguria School welcomes students who demonstrate academic potential, discipline, and a commitment to excellence. Admission is competitive and follows guidelines set by the Ministry of Education.

Admission Requirements

  • Valid KCPE results slip
  • Completed admission form (available at the school office)
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Two recent passport‑size photographs
  • Medical report signed by a certified doctor

School Uniform

All students are required to wear the official Kapenguria School uniform, which reflects discipline and unity:

  • Grey jamper
  • White shirt
  • black trousers
  • Grey tie with school logo
  • Black shoes

Boarding Facilities

Kapenguria School is a full boarding institution. Students are provided with dormitory accommodation, meals, and access to recreational facilities. Discipline and cleanliness are emphasized in all boarding houses.

Fees & Payment

School fees are payable at the beginning of each term through designated bank accounts. Parents and guardians are encouraged to clear fees promptly to ensure uninterrupted learning.

How to Apply

Prospective students and parents can collect admission forms from the school office or download them from the official website. Completed forms should be submitted with all required documents before the admission deadline.
